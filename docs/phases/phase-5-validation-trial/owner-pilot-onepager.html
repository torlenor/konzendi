Question: Do I record topic switches during normal computer work, and do I learn something useful from the result?

Before normal work on Monday

  1. Verify the v0.1.1 package with SHA256SUMS, then install it.
  2. Back up ~/.local/share/com.konzendi.app to a private location.
  3. Record the actual start time: ______________________________
  4. Confirm: data opens · Ctrl+Alt+K works over another app · Stop works · Analytics opens.
  5. Use real topics only. Do not add synthetic events after the start time.

During normal work

Record a switch when you notice it.
Use Entries for a missed or late switch.
Use the main window, quick switcher, or tray naturally.
Do not use reminder alarms or a parallel tracker.
Keep working normally. Do not create extra switches for the pilot. If logging becomes inconvenient, keep that as evidence.

Unavailable workday: record why and extend by one workday, at most to 22 September. Do not extend because logging was inconvenient.

Friday review

  1. Which logged pattern was useful, and why?
  2. Would I keep using v0.1.1 for another week without the trial?
  3. Which entry point worked best? What still caused missed entries?
  4. Did an installation, integrity, persistence, or privacy problem make the data unsafe to interpret?

Decision gates

  • Repair first: unsafe or unreliable data.
  • Redirect and repeat: fewer than 4 usable days, under 80% of known switches present after correction, or median friction above 2.
  • Continue: gates pass and at least one observation suggests a useful action or question.
  • Stop expansion: reliable and low-friction, but not useful and I would not continue.

Private: Keep the raw log, topic names, work screenshots, and identifying notes off the repository. Share only redacted counts and findings.
