# Open questions

Track questions and their decisions here, retaining resolved rows for reference. Proposals in the
[concept discussion](brainstorming/context-switching-app-concept.md) are inputs, not defaults.
When a decision is made, record it and its rationale in the relevant phase document, update
its status and outcome here, and update affected plans. Summarize the outcome briefly and link
to the decision details. A proposed option is not an accepted decision.

Question status is one of `Open`, `Investigating`, `Resolved`, or `Deferred`. For deferred
questions, record why and when to revisit them in the outcome. These describe questions;
phase status remains exclusively in the roadmap. Related phases are optional: use `—` when
none has been assigned, and link to existing phase documents when applicable.

| Q05 | What is the primary logging interaction, and how are pauses, accidental switches, missed entries, and completed work represented? | Before the tracking implementation plan is ready | Buttons, shortcut, tray, undo, and corrections are proposed. | Resolved | Selecting a topic is one action that ends the running interval and starts the next. Topics are entities with stable ids, created while logging, renameable and archivable. A pause is a distinct state that never ends automatically. Accidental switches and wrong times are corrected by `entry.revoked` and `entry.retimed` events referencing an earlier event id, folded deterministically. Missed entries are handled by a prominent elapsed time, back-dating, and inserting a past switch; nothing is automatic. Completed work is not distinguished from an interruption in this scope. The quick-access surface offers switching, pause, and undo of the last entry. | Q12 | What visual direction should the application theme take, which modes must it support, and what accessibility floor applies? | Before the theme is applied | The current styling is an undocumented default palette with literal values, no tokens, and no light/dark handling. | Resolved | "Readout": the window is an instrument panel whose elapsed reading dominates the fold, colour carries tracking state rather than decoration, and switch targets show the number key that fires them. Light and dark both ship, following the desktop setting with an in-application override stored in `localStorage`, which appends no event. The floor is WCAG 2.2 AA, measured on rendered output.
